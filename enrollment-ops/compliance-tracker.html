OEC Submission & 7-Day CMS Compliance Tracker

CMS Requirement: Enrollment applications must be submitted within 7 calendar days of application signature date. At-Risk alerts at Day 5 (configurable). Compliance Breach escalation at Day 8.
Effective Date Lifecycle: Agent enters a Requested Effective Date (always 1st of month). On CMS acceptance, a Confirmed Effective Date is returned and used for all downstream commission calculations (Initial vs. Renewal per CMS MMR). Rejected enrollments receive no confirmed date and generate no commission. Delayed CMS updates that change the confirmed date trigger automatic proration recalculation.
