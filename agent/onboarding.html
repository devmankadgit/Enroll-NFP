          AGENT CONTRACTING AGREEMENT

          This agreement is entered into between EnrollAxis, Inc. ("Carrier") and the agent identified during registration ("Agent").

          1. Appointment. Carrier appoints Agent to solicit applications for Carrier's Medicare Advantage plans in the selected territories. Appointment is effective only after Carrier approval and state appointment filing.

          2. Compliance. Agent agrees to comply with all CMS marketing guidelines, HIPAA requirements, and applicable state regulations, including Scope of Appointment rules and the 48-hour waiting period.

          3. Commissions. Carrier shall pay Agent commissions per the current commission schedule. Chargebacks apply to rapid disenrollments as described in Schedule B.

          4. Training. Agent must complete annual product and compliance training before selling in each plan year.

          5. Term & Termination. This agreement remains in effect unless terminated by either party with 30 days written notice, or immediately for cause.

          6. Confidentiality. Agent shall maintain strict confidentiality of all member, carrier, and proprietary information, during and after the term of this agreement.

          7. Records. Agent shall retain enrollment records for ten (10) years and make them available to Carrier and CMS on request.

          8. Entire Agreement. This agreement and its schedules are the entire agreement between the parties and replace all earlier agreements on this subject.
