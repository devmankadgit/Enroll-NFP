Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my portal password?
Click your profile icon → "My Profile" → "Security" tab → "Change Password". If locked out, click "Forgot Password" on the login page or call the Agent Support Hotline.
Why is my commission showing $0 for a member?
Commission is paid after the member's enrollment is fully processed and the 7-day CMS confirmation period completes. Check the member's enrollment status. If enrollment is confirmed but commission is still $0, create a support ticket under "Commission / Payment Issues".
How do I submit an HRA for a new member?
Navigate to HRA Management → click "Start HRA" next to the member's name. The HRA must be submitted within 14 days of enrollment effective date to qualify for the $25 HRA incentive.
My enrollment submission shows "Pended" — what does that mean?
A "Pended" status means the enrollment requires additional documentation or review. Check Enrollment Status for the specific pend reason. Common reasons include missing SOA, C-SNP verification pending, or incomplete beneficiary information. Address the issue and resubmit.
How do I update my payment preferences (ACH)?
Go to My Profile → "Payment" tab → "Update ACH Information". Changes take effect on the next payment cycle. You'll receive a confirmation email once updated.
When does AHIP recertification open for next plan year?
AHIP certification for the next plan year typically opens September 1 and must be completed before December 31. Check Training & Certification for your current status and deadlines.
I can't see a lead that was assigned to me
Lead assignments may take up to 15 minutes to sync. Try refreshing the page. If the lead still doesn't appear, check with your upline or create a support ticket under "Lead Management".
