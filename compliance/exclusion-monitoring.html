Investigation Workflow
Detection
Monthly automated screening against OIG-LEIE and SAM databases. Name, NPN, NPI, DOB matching with confidence scoring.
Review & Verify
Compliance officer reviews matches >50% confidence. Cross-reference DOB, SSN last 4, state licenses. Request additional documentation from agent if needed.
Auto-Hold
Matches >90% confidence trigger automatic compliance hold. All enrollments paused. Agent notified. Upline alerted. Commission payments suspended.
Resolution
False positive: Clear and restore. Confirmed match: Immediate termination, CMS notification within 30 days, enrollment reassignment.

Regulatory Requirements & Policy
CMS Requirements
  • 42 CFR § 422.503(b)(4)(vi)(C) — Monthly OIG/GSA-SAM exclusion screening for all FDRs
  • Chapter 21 MMCM — Results documented within 30 calendar days
  • Screening must include: OIG-LEIE, SAM (formerly EPLS), state Medicaid exclusion lists
  • Matches require immediate removal from patient care/billing pending investigation
  • Confirmed exclusions: Terminate within 30 days, notify CMS
EnrollAxis Policy (P-COMP-008)
  • Automated monthly screening on 1st business day of each month
  • All active agents, brokers, and contracted entities screened
  • >90% confidence: Auto-hold + immediate compliance officer review
  • 50-90% confidence: Flagged for manual review within 5 business days
  • <50% confidence: Logged, no action (monitored on next cycle)
  • 7-year retention of all screening results and investigation records
  • Quarterly attestation to CMS of ongoing screening compliance
Audit Note: EnrollAxis has maintained 100% monthly screening compliance since program inception (Jan 2024). Total screenings completed: 30. Total confirmed exclusions: 4 (all terminated within SLA). Zero CMS audit findings related to exclusion monitoring.
