Critical Business Rules:
RuleDescriptionBehavior
Eligibility Reference Date Commission eligibility is determined by the agent's active status on the application date (date enrollment form was signed/submitted) The member's actual effective date (always 1st of a future month) is NOT the reference date. System stores application date on each enrollment record for validation.
Effective = Term Date If a member's actual effective date equals their term date Member is included in the BoB but generates $0.00 commission. Ledger entry created with status "Skipped — Zero Duration".
Chargeback Handling Rapid disenrollment within configured window triggers chargeback Chargeback posted as a negative commission entry in the ledger. Member remains in the BoB even after chargeback (historical record preserved).
BoB Membership Persistence Once confirmed in the Book of Business, the member record persists Disenrollment or chargeback does not remove member from BoB. Status changes to "Termed" or "Chargedback" but record remains for compliance/audit.
Commission Calculation — Two-Step Sequence
Step 1 — Book of Business Membership Check
  • Member enters BoB at application submission
  • BoB membership maintained for ALL enrollments regardless of subsequent status
  • Requested effective date stored at application time
  • CMS Approved Date (Actual Effective Date) set upon CMS confirmation
  • Term Date = date plan coverage ends
  • Actual Effective Date & Term Date govern commission eligibility, not BoB inclusion
Step 2 — Commission Rule Application
  • Apply rules in defined sequence:
    1. Initial/Renewal classification
    2. Proration (by actual effective month)
    3. CMS standard commission calculation
    4. Override cascade (per hierarchy level)
    5. Patient activation fees (HRA & Healthy Visit)
  • Effective Date = Term Date → $0.00 commission
  • Chargebacks = negative entries; member stays in BoB
Book of Business (BoB) — Agent Transaction Ledger
The BoB is the agent's complete enrollment transaction ledger. Monthly commission calculations use the active BoB as the primary input source rather than re-deriving from raw enrollment events — ensuring calculation efficiency and consistency.
BoB Entry StateDescriptionCommission Impact
ActiveEnrolled member with active plan coverageGenerates ongoing initial or renewal commission
Disenrolled / TermedMember terminated or disenrolled from planGenerates chargeback entry (negative) if within rapid disenrollment window
Cancelled / CMS-RejectedApplication cancelled or rejected by CMSRecorded with $0.00 commission and rejection reason code
