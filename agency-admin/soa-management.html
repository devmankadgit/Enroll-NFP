Step 1: Send Consent to Member

CMS requires that members provide written consent before discussing any Medicare Advantage plan details. Send the consent form to the member using their preferred method.

Step 2: Confirm Consent Received from Member

Confirm that the member has acknowledged the scope of appointment. The member must agree to the topics before the appointment can proceed.

Step 3: Member Signature Capture

Capture the member's signature on the SOA form. This must be obtained before the scheduled appointment. Without signature, the appointment cannot proceed.

CMS Compliance: A valid SOA with member consent and signature must be obtained at least 48 hours before any in-person appointment (phone/mail appointments: before discussion begins). Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.
