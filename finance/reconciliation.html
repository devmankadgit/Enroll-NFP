Post-Payment Reconciliation & Commission Audit

  1. Finance Portal
  2. Reconciliation
Reconciliation Policy: Post-payment reconciliation auto-runs within 1-2 business days of each payment date (configurable via Admin › Recon Deadline Config). The system performs a 3-way match: Calculated Ledger ↔ Payment Batch ↔ Bank ACH/Check Settlement. Data sources include CMS Monthly Membership Reconciliation (MMR) files for disenrollment verification. ACH returns and check rejections are auto-detected and reconciled. Variance tolerance: $0.00 — all differences must be documented. Records retained 7 years. Finance sign-off required on each monthly report.
